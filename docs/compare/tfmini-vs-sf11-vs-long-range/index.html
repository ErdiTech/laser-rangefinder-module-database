TFmini vs SF11 vs long-range laser rangefinder modules for drones (2026)

Updated: Nov 2026

Single-point rangefinders used on drones fall into three published-range classes. The Benewake TFmini family is a short-range ToF class sensor, the LightWare SF11/C is a 100 m class LiDAR (end of life according to its maker), and long-range laser rangefinder modules rate 1 km or more. The tables below list every module in the dataset in each class that publishes a weight within the stated limit, so the choice can be made on range, weight, rate and interface. Data date 2026-11-17.

Rule of thumb

  • Landing, terrain following below ~10 m, obstacle stop: short-range ToF modules (high rate, a few grams).
  • Altitude hold and terrain following at survey heights: 100-600 m LiDAR modules.
  • Ranging to distant targets from a gimbal, or altitude above 600 m: long-range modules (1-10 Hz typical).
